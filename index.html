About Kazi Additive Limited

Building Self-Reliant Manufacturing & Global Technical Partnerships

Kazi Additive Limited is a progressive Bangladeshi enterprise operating at the intersection of modern animal nutrition, industrial polymer packaging, yarn, and daily consumer goods. With our corporate headquarters in Dhaka and an integrated manufacturing complex in Tangail, we serve feed mills, commercial livestock farms, textile producers, and retail consumers nationwide.

Guided by Chairman Maksuda Easmin and led by Managing Director Kazi Salah Uddin, our company pairs exclusive international formulations such as German feed additives from Dr. Eckel Animal Nutrition with disciplined domestic production.

About Kazi Additive Limited

A trusted Bangladeshi industrial enterprise bridging European agro-nutritional science with domestic manufacturing across packaging, textiles, and consumer goods.

Corporate Profile

Committed to Quality, Integrity & Sustainable Growth

Kazi Additive Limited was founded with a clear commitment: to raise the standard of animal nutrition, industrial packaging, and daily consumer essentials in Bangladesh. Headquartered in Dhaka with a modern industrial plant in Tangail, we operate across three synergistic verticals.

As an authorized partner of Dr. Eckel Animal Nutrition GmbH & Co. KG (Germany), we bring research-backed phytogenic feed additives and toxin binders to Bangladesh’s leading poultry, dairy, and aqua feed mills, while manufacturing durable PP woven bags, multi-filament yarn, and household FMCG products locally.

Our Mission

Serving farmers, businesses and families

To deliver safe, innovative, and cost-effective nutritional, industrial, and consumer solutions that empower farmers, businesses, and families nationwide.

Our Vision

A benchmark Bangladeshi industrial group

To be recognized as a benchmark Bangladeshi industrial group synonymous with international quality compliance, ethical governance, and manufacturing self-reliance.

Infrastructure & Compliance

Dhaka warehousing, Tangail production

Active member of BAFIITA (Bangladesh Agro Feed Ingredients Importers & Traders Association), operating temperature-controlled warehousing in Dhaka and high-capacity extrusion, weaving, and packaging lines in Tangail.
